**Brand idea:** Give a useful AI conversation a clear introduction and a presentation someone feels proud to share. **Primary emotion:** confidence in putting one's thinking into the world. **Rationale:** A clear promise in a calm visual system: Inter, white and charcoal, and a painted landscape give each passage room.

The name is title case in the wordmark. The shareable artifact is a lowercase **passage**: “Create a passage,” “Read the passage,” and “your passages.” Internal terminology remains **Publication**, as defined in the [glossary](../../CONTEXT.md). The singular product name and lowercase object noun have distinct roles.

## Audience, promise, and scope

This small open-source web service helps heavy AI users and builders share useful work with peers. A long conversation can hide the good ideas and ask too much of an unfamiliar reader. A title, grounded highlights, considered presentation, and an accessible saved conversation make that work easier to approach.

The current product accepts supported public ChatGPT, Codex, and Claude links, generates a read-only title and highlights, offers five curated card styles, and publishes a share link with saved conversation text and an original-source link. The browser, CLI, and agent skill support the same workflow. Review before publication and content-specific highlights support the promise; personal branding, editable summaries, collections, audience growth, and perfect media fidelity are not current claims. See the [MVP scope](../../MVP_PLAN.md).

## Voice and naming

Lead with a clear benefit in ordinary language. Pair the direct headline with the calm visual system, and keep supporting copy and controls concrete, welcoming, and respectful of the work being shared.

| Do | Avoid | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the passage | Inspect the publication | Use the agreed customer noun. |
| Preview first. Publish when you’re ready. | Perfect your summary before sharing | The generated text is reviewed, not edited. |

Useful retained interface copy: **Public conversation link**, **Paste a public link…**, **Preparing…**, and **ChatGPT, Codex & Claude. No account needed.** The primary creation label in this preview is **Create a passage**. Errors and progress messages should state what happened and the next available action plainly.

**Accepted name:** **Passage** is fixed as the product name. Use **passage** for one shareable artifact and **passages** for several. The [September 11 domain shortlist](../domain-search-2026-09-11.md) records then-current availability and prices, not ownership or a selected domain.

## Typography, color, and composition

Use **Inter 400** for body copy and highlights, **Inter 500** for headings and actions, and **Inter 600** for the wordmark. Keep the present clear sans-serif hierarchy and generous spacing. The desktop wordmark reference is 21 px with −0.8 px tracking; the hero reference is 68 px with a tight 1.055 line-height. Adjust the review composition to fit the approved copy without changing it. Local font inputs and the license are retained from [round 06](../round-06/assets/fonts/inter-LICENSE.txt).

| Token    | Value   | Role and pairing                                 |
| -------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| White    | #FFFFFF | Dominant page and card surface                   |
| Charcoal | #171717 | Headline, wordmark, mark, and main text on White |
| Muted    | #737373 | Supporting copy and quiet labels on White        |

Keep the landscape substantial behind the white example passage, with quiet framing, a centered introduction, and readable content. Artwork colors belong to the image rather than a new accent palette. The product identity should frame the shared work with restraint.

**Accepted icon: Open passage.** The [native master](assets/marks/open-passage-mark.svg), also exported as the [active preview mark](assets/marks/passage-mark.svg), is a solid split P with a narrow open channel. It replaces the outlined-page icon in this preview. The 32 × 32 viewBox contains two filled paths: a vertical spine and a separated open bowl. The mark was inspected at 16, 24, and 32 px and in reverse. Use Charcoal on White in the review lockup. Canonical clear-space and favicon rules are part of the accepted export work. The [icon study](icon-study.png) records the Excerpt and Paragraph alternatives considered within this Passage direction.
