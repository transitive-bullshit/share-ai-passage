# Folio — The considered edition

**Status:** Retained proposed identity in round 05. The user rejects Index, Cut paper, and After hours and keeps original Folio for comparison with the current Passage homepage identity. Folio’s selected elements remain the reference; no complete canonical identity is accepted yet.

## Identity and exact copy

- **Name:** Folio. No new naming research in this round.
- **Brand idea:** Useful thinking deserves a considered public form.
- **Main one-liner:** Your thinking, worth sharing.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** Give good work a good form.
- **Character:** Considered. Independent. Proud.
- **Primary emotion:** Pride in presenting useful thinking with care.
- **Rationale:** The retained Folio identity: considered paper, Instrument Sans, sculptural book imagery, and a warm ribbon accent on the page-turn mark.
- **Primary action:** Create a share

## Retained system

- **Typography:** Instrument Sans normal 400 for supporting copy and normal 500 for the wordmark, headline, share title, and actions. No serif or italic treatment.
- **Mark:** Page turn. The retained Folio page-turn geometry, used in the warm accent color. Preserve the three curved paper planes. Use Ribbon #B85540, as requested; the geometry is unchanged.
- **Imagery:** [Existing sculptural book](assets/folio-hero.png). Alt text: A fan of ivory book pages with a muted vermilion ribbon. Keep its material character and generous empty space.
- **Layout and expression:** Quiet editorial presentation, careful hierarchy, warm paper, and a low-coverage ribbon accent. The shared artifact remains the focal point; branding is a small signature.
- **Tradeoff:** Folio carries more literary and authorial association than Passage. The explicit AI-chat descriptor keeps the product’s immediate use clear.
