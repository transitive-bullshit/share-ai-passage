# Folio / Ribbon — A personal signature

**Status:** Proposed round 03 refinement, not an accepted complete identity. Folio’s font, palette, and book concept are retained from explicit positive feedback. The previous Folio and Waymark icons were rejected. Folio is not being renamed; Waymark remains a working name, and Along is a related naming experiment. No name-availability research was performed in this round.

## Copy and direction

- **Main one-liner:** Your thinking, worth sharing.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** Give good work a good form.
- **Character:** Considered. Personal. Proud.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Emphasis:** Tests how little product branding Folio needs. The approved type, palette, and book world stay in place while the share takes more of the frame. A small folded ribbon becomes the signature.
- **Tradeoff:** The wordmark-first treatment is quieter, but the ribbon’s recognition will depend on consistent, restrained use.

## Mark and visual rules

- **Mark name:** Ribbon signature.
- **Mark concept:** A folded ribbon serves as the small signature. The full name leads without a separate icon.
- **Lockup:** Primary lockup: Folio wordmark without a separate leading icon. The native ribbon is a secondary signature and a candidate for a future favicon; no final favicon is produced or accepted in this round.
- **Type:** Instrument Sans only, normal 400 for supporting text and normal 500 for the wordmark, headline, share title, and actions. Reuse the local WOFF files and licenses; no serif or italic styling.
- **Palette:** Keep broad fields calm and let the share artifact dominate. Product marks, mantras, and metadata are supporting details.
- **Imagery:** Reuse [assets/folio-hero.png](assets/folio-hero.png) from the earlier exploration. Alt text: A fan of ivory book pages with a muted vermilion ribbon. Original generation details are in the [earlier provenance](../round-01/assets/midjourney-provenance.md). Different framing is a composition decision, not a new image generation.
