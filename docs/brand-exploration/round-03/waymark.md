# Waymark / Open trail — A public trail of learning

**Status:** Proposed round 03 refinement, not an accepted complete identity. Folio’s font, palette, and book concept are retained from explicit positive feedback. The previous Folio and Waymark icons were rejected. Folio is not being renamed; Waymark remains a working name, and Along is a related naming experiment. No name-availability research was performed in this round.

## Copy and direction

- **Main one-liner:** Share your thinking. Leave a trail.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** A little shared today. A wider path tomorrow.
- **Character:** Curious. Unhurried. Discoverable.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Emphasis:** Refines the liked public trail/breadcrumb idea without changing the working name yet. A compact cairn replaces the rejected growing-square mark: three low stones suggest a small sign that someone passed this way.
- **Tradeoff:** Waymark remains uncertain as a name and can suggest wayfinding. The waypoint is a metaphor for sharing over time, not a map or discovery feature.

## Mark and visual rules

- **Mark name:** A small cairn.
- **Mark concept:** Three low, rounded stones make a waypoint. A small piece of evidence that someone passed this way.
- **Lockup:** Primary lockup: a compact cairn with the Waymark wordmark. Keep the three solid rounded stones low and broad; avoid a pyramid, upward-diagonal cadence, increasing blocks, arrows, pins, and charts.
- **Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans only, normal 400 for supporting text and normal 500 for the wordmark, headline, share title, and actions. Reuse the local WOFF files and licenses; no serif or italic styling.
- **Palette:** Keep broad fields calm and let the share artifact dominate. Product marks, mantras, and metadata are supporting details.
- **Imagery:** Reuse [assets/waymark-hero.png](assets/waymark-hero.png) from the earlier exploration. Alt text: A sparse trail of small ochre pebbles and ivory paper fragments branching over pale sand. Original generation details are in the [earlier provenance](../round-02/assets/midjourney-provenance.md). Different framing is a composition decision, not a new image generation.
