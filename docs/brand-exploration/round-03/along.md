# Along / Breadcrumbs — A little shared along the way

**Status:** Proposed round 03 refinement, not an accepted complete identity. Folio’s font, palette, and book concept are retained from explicit positive feedback. The previous Folio and Waymark icons were rejected. Folio is not being renamed; Waymark remains a working name, and Along is a related naming experiment. No name-availability research was performed in this round.

## Copy and direction

- **Main one-liner:** Share your thinking. Leave a trail.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** Share as you go. Let the work add up.
- **Character:** Open. Personal. In progress.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Emphasis:** A related naming and system experiment for the trail concept. Along pairs the public trail with Folio’s liked Instrument Sans and exact palette, making the idea feel personal and unhurried.
- **Tradeoff:** Along is approachable but less descriptive by itself. Keep the explicit AI-chat descriptor nearby. The new name has not been checked for availability.

## Mark and visual rules

- **Mark name:** An open trail.
- **Mark concept:** A gently curving route and a separate next step keep the trail open. The shape stays human and unhurried.
- **Lockup:** Primary lockup: a gently curving continuous route and detached next step with the Along wordmark. Keep the gesture open and quiet; it must not resemble an angular waveform, growth chart, or navigation control.
- **Type:** Instrument Sans only, normal 400 for supporting text and normal 500 for the wordmark, headline, share title, and actions. Reuse the local WOFF files and licenses; no serif or italic styling.
- **Palette:** Keep broad fields calm and let the share artifact dominate. Product marks, mantras, and metadata are supporting details.
- **Imagery:** Reuse [assets/waymark-hero.png](assets/waymark-hero.png) from the earlier exploration. Alt text: A sparse trail of small ochre pebbles and ivory paper fragments branching over pale sand. Original generation details are in the [earlier provenance](../round-02/assets/midjourney-provenance.md). Different framing is a composition decision, not a new image generation.
