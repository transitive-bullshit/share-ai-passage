# Round 03 proposed native marks

These are native SVG geometry masters, all with a 100 × 100 viewBox and #2A2824 color for deterministic recoloring. No raster image generation was used.

- **Folio / pages:** three asymmetric curved paper planes echo the existing sculptural page-fan photograph. Open negative channels keep the planes separate and let the center fold read at small sizes. This is an emblem exploration, not a file icon or letterform.
- **Folio / ribbon:** two folded, overlapping ribbon planes create a compact diagonal tab. A thin negative seam separates the returning fold. This is a secondary emblem; the primary identity can remain wordmark-only.
- **Waymark / a small cairn:** three close, low, asymmetrical stone forms make a compact solid waypoint. Broad simple silhouettes support recognition at small sizes; the middle stone is widest so the stack avoids a regular pyramid or progression of growth bars.
- **Along / an open trail:** a single gently curved open route is followed by one separated dash of equal weight. This reuses the first-pass Waymark geometry; the earlier rectilinear Along steps were discarded because they read as a technical waveform.

All four are proposed for comparison and have not been accepted. Root composition renders are the intended visual review surface.
