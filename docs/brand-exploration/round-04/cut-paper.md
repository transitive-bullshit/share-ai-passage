# Folio / Cut paper — A little more character

**Status:** Proposed round 04 application of the Folio identity. The user retains the first Folio / Pages base and requests three significant departures to compare with it. This is continued exploration, not a selected canonical identity or three new brands. Waymark, Along, and the standalone Folio / Ribbon direction are dropped from the active set.

## Copy and emphasis

- **Main one-liner:** Your thinking, with character.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** A little personality. A clear idea.
- **Character:** Tactile. Personal. Open.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Creative argument:** Give the same Folio identity a materially different medium: tactile cut-paper collage and an asymmetric, personal presentation. The share stays readable while its setting carries more character.
- **Composition:** Use broad simplified paper shapes and restrained rusty color, with native upright type. The collage surrounds one clear share rather than becoming a loud promotional poster. Avoid stickers, visual clutter, and Relay-like saturation.
- **Tradeoff:** The more expressive material can compete with the ideas if it grows too busy. Preserve a calm reading field and quiet product branding.

## Shared identity and visual treatment

- **Name and type:** Folio, with Instrument Sans throughout. Normal 400 for supporting copy and normal 500 for the wordmark, headline, share title, and actions.
- **Mark:** Page turn. The retained Folio page-turn geometry, used in the warm accent color. All four treatments use the same retained native page-turn geometry. Differences in layout, material, mood, and card presentation are the exploration; these are not four new logo proposals.
- **Mark color here:** Rust #B85540. The baseline’s required icon color is Ribbon #B85540; the warm-dark treatment uses its lighter Copper accent.
- **Brand hierarchy:** Product branding remains small. The shared artifact and its useful content carry the main visual emphasis.
- **Imagery:** The [Cut paper hero](assets/cut-paper-hero.png) is an imagegen style-transfer edit of the existing Folio image. The exact reference, prompt, output, and inspection notes are in [Cut paper provenance](assets/cut-paper-provenance.md).
- **Hero/accessibility description:** A sculptural paper collage of ivory pages and a rust ribbon, framed by muted blue and terracotta paper.
