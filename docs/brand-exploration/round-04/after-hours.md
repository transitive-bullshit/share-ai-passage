# Folio / After hours — Thoughtful work, warmly shared

**Status:** Proposed round 04 application of the Folio identity. The user retains the first Folio / Pages base and requests three significant departures to compare with it. This is continued exploration, not a selected canonical identity or three new brands. Waymark, Along, and the standalone Folio / Ribbon direction are dropped from the active set.

## Copy and emphasis

- **Main one-liner:** Thought through. Ready to share.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** Give good thinking room.
- **Character:** Focused. Warm. Deliberate.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Creative argument:** Test a warm evening atmosphere. A cream share becomes the brightest object against dark walnut, with the book and warm light suggesting focused, considered work.
- **Composition:** Create depth through warm physical light, dark brown surroundings, and a bright paper artifact. Keep the shared content in the foreground. Avoid green/technical fields, neon, terminal references, and dark cards that lose the useful text.
- **Tradeoff:** This changes atmosphere substantially while preserving the Folio geometry and type. It can feel like a reading product unless the explicit chat-to-share descriptor remains prominent.

## Shared identity and visual treatment

- **Name and type:** Folio, with Instrument Sans throughout. Normal 400 for supporting copy and normal 500 for the wordmark, headline, share title, and actions.
- **Mark:** Page turn. The retained Folio page-turn geometry, used in the warm accent color. All four treatments use the same retained native page-turn geometry. Differences in layout, material, mood, and card presentation are the exploration; these are not four new logo proposals.
- **Mark color here:** Copper #D28C73. The baseline’s required icon color is Ribbon #B85540; the warm-dark treatment uses its lighter Copper accent.
- **Brand hierarchy:** Product branding remains small. The shared artifact and its useful content carry the main visual emphasis.
- **Imagery:** The [After hours hero](assets/after-hours-hero.png) comes from one completed standard four-image Midjourney batch. Candidate 3 was selected, visually inspected, and saved as a 1344 × 896 PNG. The exact submitted prompt, effective parameters, result link, download source, and selection notes are recorded in [After hours provenance](assets/after-hours-provenance.md).
- **Hero/accessibility description:** An ivory book and rust ribbon on dark walnut under warm evening light.
