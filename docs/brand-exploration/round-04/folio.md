# Folio / Baseline — The considered edition

**Status:** Proposed round 04 application of the Folio identity. The user retains the first Folio / Pages base and requests three significant departures to compare with it. This is continued exploration, not a selected canonical identity or three new brands. Waymark, Along, and the standalone Folio / Ribbon direction are dropped from the active set.

## Copy and emphasis

- **Main one-liner:** Your thinking, worth sharing.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** Give good work a good form.
- **Character:** Considered. Independent. Proud.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Creative argument:** Preserve the first Folio / Pages direction as the reference: same Instrument Sans, Paper/Ink/Ribbon palette, sculptural book, and page-turn geometry. The requested change is the icon color: Ribbon #B85540.
- **Composition:** The familiar balance of headline, sculptural book image, and readable share remains. This option provides a stable comparison point rather than another new direction.
- **Tradeoff:** Folio is the chosen base for further exploration. Its whole final identity and this round’s preferred application have not been selected.

## Shared identity and visual treatment

- **Name and type:** Folio, with Instrument Sans throughout. Normal 400 for supporting copy and normal 500 for the wordmark, headline, share title, and actions.
- **Mark:** Page turn. The retained Folio page-turn geometry, used in the warm accent color. All four treatments use the same retained native page-turn geometry. Differences in layout, material, mood, and card presentation are the exploration; these are not four new logo proposals.
- **Mark color here:** Ribbon #B85540. The baseline’s required icon color is Ribbon #B85540; the warm-dark treatment uses its lighter Copper accent.
- **Brand hierarchy:** Product branding remains small. The shared artifact and its useful content carry the main visual emphasis.
- **Imagery:** Reuse the existing [Folio hero](assets/folio-hero.png). See [round 01 Midjourney provenance](../round-01/assets/midjourney-provenance.md).
- **Hero/accessibility description:** A fan of ivory book pages with a muted vermilion ribbon.
