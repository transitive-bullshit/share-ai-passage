# Folio — The considered edition

**Status:** Proposed round 02 exploration. No direction is accepted. Names are provisional; availability was not checked in this round. Earlier [round 01 naming notes](../round-01/naming-notes.md) remain historical context for Folio and Commonplace.

## Copy and emphasis

- **Main one-liner:** Your thinking, worth sharing.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** Give good work a good form.
- **Character:** Considered. Independent. Proud.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Creative argument:** Keep the sculptural book and paper metaphor the user liked, replace every serif with a calm contemporary sans, and give the share artifact more space than the product wordmark. The feeling is pride in work presented with care.
- **Tradeoff:** The book can suggest a writing tool; the explicit AI-chat descriptor keeps the category clear.

## Visual system

- **Type:** Instrument Sans throughout. Normal 400 for descriptions and highlights; normal 500 for headlines, share titles, wordmark, and controls. No serif, italic, or monospace treatment.
- **Hierarchy:** Shared content dominates. Keep the service wordmark, mark, labels, and decorative devices visually subordinate. Distinguish roles with spacing and measured weight rather than oversized branding.
- **Imagery:** A sculptural fan of ivory pages with a restrained warm ribbon. Preserve the tactile object and generous breathing room.
- **Mark:** Working, small, simple native geometry. Use it as a signature beside the artifact, never a competing focal point.

## Fixed brief and boundaries

The user rejected Trace’s green/technical expression and Relay’s boldness; they liked Folio’s conceptual book and restraint and Commonplace’s book/plant and calm, but rejected serif typography in both. Round 02 keeps copy simple and explicit and reduces product branding so the shared work stands out. Frame and Kindred add two restrained alternatives; Waymark explicitly tests the expanding public trail metaphor.

Current functionality is public ChatGPT/Codex/Claude input, generated highlights, a curated style choice, and a published share link with a readable saved conversation. The imagery and language do not introduce custom-brand controls, editable summaries, accounts, analytics, profiles, collections, distribution, or audience-growth guarantees. Read the [working brief](../brief.md) for context.
