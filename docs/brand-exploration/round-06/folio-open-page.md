# Folio / Open page — The considered edition

**Status:** Proposed round 06 hybrid. The user requested two alternatives combining Passage’s color scheme, typography, and general visual direction with Folio’s name and copy. Original Folio and original Passage remain unchanged references. This is a proposal under `docs/brand-exploration`, not current project guidance or an accepted canonical identity.

## Name and exact copy

- **Name:** Folio. “Open page” is an exploration label, not a new product name.
- **Main one-liner:** Your thinking, worth sharing.
- **Descriptive one-liner:** Turn your AI chats into links you’ll be proud to share.
- **Mantra:** Give good work a good form.
- **Character:** Considered. Independent. Proud.
- **Primary action:** Create a share
- **Rationale:** Folio’s words and page-turn mark meet Passage’s neutral Inter system. A left-aligned introduction and a larger white share create a more direct, asymmetric composition.

All hybrid product naming and copy come from Folio. Do not leave Passage-specific “Create a passage,” “A passage from…,” the Passage headline, or its footer mantra in these hybrid specimens. Ordinary conversation/share labels remain appropriate. Original Passage keeps its own wording.

## Visual system and composition

- **Typography:** Inter throughout. Inter normal 600 for the wordmark, 500 for headlines, share titles, and actions, and 400 for body/highlights. Reuse the existing local fonts; no Instrument Sans, serif, or italic treatment in the hybrid.
- **Composition:** A left-aligned introduction beside a larger white share creates an asymmetric composition. The painted landscape remains a substantial, recognizable frame around the artifact, not a tiny decorative strip. Retain the neutral Inter system and open spacing; change the arrangement and mark rather than invent another visual world.
- **Mark:** Page turn; explicit rendering style `page-turn`. Folio’s retained page-turn geometry in Passage’s Charcoal, paired with an Inter wordmark.
- **Native geometry:** The retained Folio silhouette of three curved paper planes, recolored Charcoal #171717. Preserve its native paths without a ribbon accent, gradients, or outline conversion.
- **Mark color:** Monochrome Charcoal #171717. No red/rust/ribbon accent in either hybrid.
- **Imagery:** Reuse [the Passage landscape](assets/passage-landscape.png), the existing 1672 × 941 painted-lake asset. Alt text: A soft impressionistic lake landscape with water lilies, trees, and distant misty hills.
- **Hierarchy:** The useful share is the main object. Keep the Folio wordmark and icon modest; use white space, typography, and the landscape rather than extra branding decoration.
- **Tradeoff:** This retains more of Folio’s own emblem while borrowing Passage’s calm visual system. The arrangement is a new hybrid composition, not an unchanged homepage extraction.
