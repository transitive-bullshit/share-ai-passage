# Round 06 asset provenance

This round reuses the two round 05 identity systems. No new images, fonts, or logo geometry were generated. See the [round 05 source record](../../round-05/assets/provenance.md) for original homepage references, image hashes, and font versions.

## Reused images and fonts

- Folio retains its sculptural book PNG, Instrument Sans 400/500 WOFF files, and warm page-turn mark.
- Passage retains its painted landscape PNG, Inter 400/500/600 WOFF files, and outlined-page mark.
- Both new hybrids use that same Passage landscape and Inter files, with Passage's white/charcoal/gray interface colors. Both use Folio's name, headline, descriptor, mantra, primary action, and share-copy labels.
- The original Folio and Passage layouts remain in the comparison. Their round metadata changes; the underlying identity choices do not.
